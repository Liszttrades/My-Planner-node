QUICK GUIDE

Canvas controls

Add a note, checklist or image using the toolbar. Drag the top of a card to move it; double-click a card to edit.

Connect cards by clicking the small side handle, then another card. Click a connection to remove it.

Select by holding the right mouse button and dragging a box. Drag a selected card to move the group. Ctrl/⌘ A selects all; Escape clears selection.

Explore by dragging empty canvas or holding Space. Wheel to zoom · Shift + wheel to pan; Ctrl/⌘ + scroll to zoom. Use Fit to see everything.

Images can be uploaded, dropped or pasted. Work saves in this browser. Export backups to keep a portable copy.

Rename board

This removes the board and its cards. You can undo until the page is reloaded.
